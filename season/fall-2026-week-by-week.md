# Fall 2026 study plan, week by week (through Christmas break)

Four 10-minute q-time sessions a week: Monday terms, Tuesday reading
check, Wednesday history or prizes, Thursday unseen passage. Every session
opens with a 30-second "Nobel of the day" (one laureate, year, country, one
work). Reading, timed tests, and essays happen outside q-time.

Assumptions to adjust: Thanksgiving week (Nov 23-27) has no practice; the
last practice week before Christmas is Dec 14-17. Terms batches are the
221 Tier 1 terms from `terms/most-tested-terms.md`, grouped by family;
period and group names are on Wednesdays instead. Thursday poems are all
public domain and can be printed.

| Day | Block |
|---|---|

metaphorical, conceit, epic simile, dead metaphor, personification,
reification, metonymy, synecdoche, metalepsis, allusion, biblical allusion,
kenning, controlling image, symbol, allegory.
