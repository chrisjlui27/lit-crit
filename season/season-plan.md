# 2026-27 Season Plan

Today is mid-September 2026. Invitational A meets start in early-to-mid
January, District is in late March / early April, Regional in late April,
and State in mid-May `[verify all dates in season/calendar.md]`. That gives
roughly 16 weeks to the first real test and about 28 weeks to District.

## Goals

- Every competitor has read all three reading-list works twice before
  Invitational A: once straight through, once with notes.
- Every competitor has covered the full terms glossary once by winter break
  and is on second-pass review by Invitational B.
- Every competitor takes a timed, full-length test at least every two weeks
  from December on, and weekly from February on.
- Team score (top three individuals) trends up test over test. Track it.

## Phase 0: Launch (now -> end of September)

- Recruit. Target four to six students so there is a full team of three
  plus alternates. Any student who reads for pleasure is a candidate.
- Fix the weekly meeting time and put it on the calendar. One 45-60 minute
  meeting a week is the floor; two is better from January on.
- Order materials:
  - Required editions of the three reading-list works, one per student.
  - Harmon, *A Handbook to Literature* (the UIL glossary source), at least
    one team copy.
  - Prior-year UIL tests (UIL store or Hexco) for diagnostics and practice.
  - Current UIL Literary Criticism Handbook and the Nobel / Pulitzer lists
    from the UIL Lit Crit page.
- Baseline diagnostic: give last year's District or Regional test, timed.
  Record per-part scores in `practice/scores.csv`. This is the "before"
  number.
- Brief students on the format using `contest/format.md`.

## Phase 1: Read and build (October -> Thanksgiving)

Weekly cadence:

- Reading: assigned chunk of the novel or drama with a short reading-check
  (5 items) at the meeting. Poems: two or three per week, read aloud, one
  close-reading question each.
- Terms: one batch from `terms/` per week (about 25-35 terms). Quiz the
  previous week's batch before introducing the new one.
- History: one period or movement per week from `history/periods.md`, plus
  a slice of the prize lists.
- Every fourth week: a half-length timed practice (Parts 1 and 3 only) to
  keep the clock in view.

Milestones:

- Mid-October: novel finished (first pass).
- Early November: drama finished (first pass).
- Thanksgiving: all poems read once; terms batches A-H done.

## Phase 2: Deepen and time (December -> Invitational A)

- Second pass on all three works, this time with the UIL question style in
  mind: character, structure, allusion, verse form, key speeches, who said
  what to whom, first and last lines.
- Finish the terms glossary. Start second-pass review using error logs.
- Full-length timed test every two weeks. Log every miss in
  `practice/error-logs/`.
- Essay: one timed tie-breaker essay (20 minutes) every two weeks, scored
  with `essay/rubric.md`.
- Invitational A: treat as a real diagnostic. Debrief within a week.

## Phase 3: Sharpen (Invitational B -> District)

- Weekly full-length timed test. Rotate prior-year tests; save the most
  recent for the final two weeks.
- Meeting time shifts toward review: go over every missed item, and have the
  student who got it right explain it.
- Targeted review from error logs: whatever part is dragging gets the extra
  meeting time.
- Essay weekly. Students trade essays and score each other with the rubric.
- Two weeks before District: dress rehearsal on the most recent unseen test,
  same room, same time of day if possible.

## Phase 4: Advance (Regional -> State)

- Same weekly rhythm, smaller group. Reading-list depth matters more at
  Regional and State: line-level detail on poems, act-and-scene detail on
  the drama, chapter-level detail on the novel.
- Prize lists: full pass on Nobel and Pulitzer winners of the last 30 years
  plus the famous older ones.

## What to track

- `practice/scores.csv`: per-part scores for every timed test.
- `practice/error-logs/`: one file per test with each missed item, the
  category, and the fix.
- `team/goals.md`: a target score per student per meet, revised after each
  test.
