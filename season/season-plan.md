# 2026-27 Season Plan

Today is late September 2026. Practice is four 10-minute q-time sessions a
week, Monday through Thursday, on campus. That is about 40 minutes a week
of face time, chopped into short blocks, so the plan is built on daily
micro-drills and independent reading, with the long work (full timed
tests, essays) scheduled separately.

Key dates (see `season/calendar.md`): Invitational A in January, District
Lit Crit on April 2 at Mt. Vernon, Regional April 24 at TJC, State May
16-18 at UT Austin. About 15 weeks to Invitational A and 26 to District.

## Goals

- Every competitor has read the six A/B/District Hawthorne stories, the
  twelve Bradstreet poems, and *Lost in Yonkers* twice before Invitational
  A: once straight through, once with notes.
- Every competitor knows the 221 Tier 1 terms (tested five or more times
  since 2009) with an example each before Invitational A, and is through
  Tier 2 by Invitational B.
- Every competitor takes a full-length timed test at least every two weeks
  from December on, and weekly from February on.
- Team score (top three individuals) trends up test over test.

## The weekly q-time rotation

Ten minutes is one drill. Do one thing per day, and do it every week.

| Day | Block | What happens in ten minutes |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Terms | 8-item quiz on last week's terms batch (2 min), go over misses (3 min), hand out this week's batch of 15-20 terms with one example each (5 min) |
| Tuesday | Reading list | 5-item reading check on the assigned story, poem, or act (4 min), discuss the two most-missed (4 min), assign the next chunk (2 min) |
| Wednesday | History and prizes | One period or group, or one decade of a prize list: 5 flashcards in, 5-item quiz out |
| Thursday | Unseen passage | One short poem or paragraph, 4 Part-3-style items, timed 6 minutes, key on the board for the last 4 |

What ten minutes cannot hold, and where it goes instead:

- Full-length timed tests (90 minutes): one after-school or weekend
  sitting every two weeks from December, weekly from February. If that is
  impossible, split a test across two sittings of Parts 1-2 and Part 3
  plus essay, timed to the same per-part budget.
- Tie-breaker essays (20 minutes): assigned as homework from a prompt in
  `essay/prompts.md`, turned in, scored with the rubric, returned Thursday.
- Reading: homework. The Tuesday check keeps it honest.
- Test debriefs: the Monday after a timed test, replace the terms block
  with a run through the ten most-missed items across the team, and log
  everything in `practice/error-logs/`.

## Phase 0: Launch (now to Oct 9)

- Recruit four to six students so there is a team of three plus alternates.
- Get every student into Tabroom and Speechwire.
- Order books: the Hawthorne stories (any unabridged printing), Bradstreet
  (Belknap or any unabridged text of each poem plus the Meditations
  site), *Lost in Yonkers* (Concord acting edition or Random House), and
  one team copy of Harmon, *A Handbook to Literature*, 12th ed.
- Baseline diagnostic: the 2026 Invitational A test in `resources/` is a
  complete test with key. Give Parts 1 and 3 timed (45 minutes) as the
  baseline; skip Part 2 since it is last year's list. Record scores in
  `practice/scores.csv`.
- Brief students on the format with `contest/format.md`. Two facts to say
  out loud on day one: the essay is required or you are disqualified, and
  Parts 2 and 3 are 70 of the 100 points.

## Phase 1: Read and build (Oct 12 to Nov 20)

Reading assignments by Tuesday check:

| Week of | Reading due |
|---|---|
| Oct 12 | "The Ambitious Guest", "Dr. Heidegger's Experiment" |
| Oct 19 | "The Birthmark", "The Celestial Railroad" |
| Oct 26 | "The Artist of the Beautiful", "Earth's Holocaust" |
| Nov 2 | *Lost in Yonkers* Act One |
| Nov 9 | *Lost in Yonkers* Act Two |
| Nov 16 | Bradstreet: Prologue, Author to Her Book, To My Dear and Loving Husband, Letter to Her Husband |

Terms: Tier 1 in batches of about 18 a week (Mondays), twelve batches,
finishing by mid-January. Start with the top of `terms/most-tested-terms.md`.

History (Wednesdays): Colonial, Revolutionary and Early National, American
Romantic (Hawthorne and Bradstreet's own periods first), then Pulitzer
Fiction 1918-1940 and Drama 1918-1938, the Invitational A windows.

## Phase 2: Deepen and time (Nov 30 to Invitational A)

| Week of | Reading due |
|---|---|
| Nov 30 | Bradstreet: Flesh and Spirit, Vanity of All Worldly Things, Before the Birth, In Reference to her Children |
| Dec 7 | Bradstreet: Queen Elizabeth, To Her Father, By Night, For Deliverance from a Fever |
| Dec 14 | Meditations 1-40 |
| Jan 4 | Meditations 41-77; second pass on all Hawthorne group 1 |
| Jan 11 | second pass on *Lost in Yonkers* and all poems |

- First full-length timed test the first week of December, then every two
  weeks. Use prior-year tests (UIL store, Hexco) for Parts 1 and 3; use
  `/practice-test` for Part 2 sets on this year's list.
- Essay every two weeks as homework.
- Wednesdays move to Pulitzer Poetry 1922-1942 and the Nobel list by decade.
- Invitational A: treat as a real diagnostic. Debrief the Monday after.

## Phase 3: Sharpen (Invitational B to District, Apr 2)

- Weekly full-length timed test. Save the two most recent tests for the
  final two weeks.
- Tuesdays shift from reading checks to item review: the student who got
  it right explains it.
- Wednesdays: Pulitzer windows for B and District (Fiction 1941-1991,
  Poetry 1943-1983, Drama 1939-1985).
- Terms: Tier 2, then error-log review.
- Essay weekly, peer-scored with the rubric.
- Two weeks before District (week of Mar 15): dress rehearsal on an unseen
  full test, same time of day if possible. The week of Mar 30 is County
  Show week and the Monday academics day; Lit Crit is Thursday Apr 2.

## Phase 4: Advance (District to State)

- Hawthorne group 2 (the six Region/State stories) must be read by Apr 10.
  Start them in March for anyone likely to advance.
- Pulitzer windows for Region (Fiction 1992-2012, Poetry 1984-2001, Drama
  1987-2006) and State (2013-2026, 2002-2026, 2007-2026).
- Line-level detail on the poems, scene-level on the play, and the
  Meditations by number.

## What to track

- `practice/scores.csv`: per-part scores for every timed test.
- `practice/error-logs/`: one file per test per student code.
- `team/goals.md`: a target score per student per meet.
