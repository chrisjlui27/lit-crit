# Literary history and prize lists (Part 1)

About 10-12 Part 1 items are literary history and about 8 are pulled from
the Nobel Prize in Literature and Pulitzer Prize (Fiction, Poetry, Drama)
recipient lists that UIL publishes. Download the current lists from the UIL
Literary Criticism page and save them here as `nobel.csv`, `pulitzer-
fiction.csv`, `pulitzer-poetry.csv`, `pulitzer-drama.csv` with columns
`year,winner,work,note`. Do not build these lists from memory.

## Periods to cover (one per week in Phase 1)

See `history/periods.md`. Each period note should have: dates, defining
traits, five to ten major authors with one signature work each, and the
term(s) the glossary associates with it.

## Prize-list item styles

- "Which of the following won the Pulitzer Prize for Fiction in 19xx?"
- "Which author is a Nobel laureate?"
- "Which work by X won the Pulitzer Prize for Drama?"
- Matching author to work among five options where all five are real.

## Drilling approach

- Week by week, cover one decade of each list.
- Prioritize: all Nobel laureates who wrote in English; every Pulitzer
  Drama winner (short list, heavily tested); Pulitzer Fiction winners since
  1980 plus the canonical earlier ones; Pulitzer Poetry winners who also
  appear in the glossary or anthologies.
- Make flashcards with the work on one side and author, prize, and year on
  the other.
