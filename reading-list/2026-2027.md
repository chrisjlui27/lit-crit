## Study notes

One notes file per work in this folder:

- `hawthorne.md`: per story, a summary, characters, setting, narrator,
  key symbols, and quotable lines with page numbers.
- `bradstreet.md`: per poem, form, meter, rhyme scheme, speaker, situation,
  turn, key images, and the first line.
- `lost-in-yonkers.md`: act-and-scene summary, cast and relationships,
  who says what to whom, stage directions that matter.

## What Part 2 items look like

From the 2026 Invitational A test (last season's list was *The Iceman
Cometh*, *To Kill a Mockingbird*, and Shakespeare's sonnets), the twenty
items split roughly five drama, seven fiction, eight poetry. Two poems were
printed in full with line numbers, and four items on each asked about
devices in specific lines (metaphor, apostrophe, elision, controlling
image, asyndeton, polyptoton, inversion, metonymy). Expect the same for
Bradstreet: know the poems, and know the device vocabulary well enough to
find it in a given line.

Fiction and drama items were about who said what to whom, occupations,
place names, quoted descriptions, and the device in a quoted sentence.
