## Study notes plan

Create one notes file per work in this folder as reading starts:

- `novel.md`: chapter-by-chapter summary, characters with one-line
  descriptions, first and last lines, epigraphs, narrative point of view,
  setting, key symbols, memorable quotations with page numbers.
- `drama.md`: act-and-scene summary, cast with relationships, who says the
  famous lines and to whom, verse vs. prose, stage directions that matter,
  any songs or letters within the play.
- `poems.md`: for each poem, form and meter, rhyme scheme, speaker,
  situation, turn, key images, and the first line (UIL items often quote a
  line and ask which poem it is from).

## Typical Part 2 item types

- Which character says or does X.
- Setting, time, or sequence of events.
- Identify the poem from a quoted line; identify the form of a poem.
