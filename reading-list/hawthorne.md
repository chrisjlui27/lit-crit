## Cross-story threads worth an item

- Puritan New England settings and the historical figures Hawthorne
  names in them.
- Allegory and the "moral" Hawthorne's narrators state or withhold.
- Scientists and artists who destroy what they perfect.
- Veils, marks, and other visible signs of hidden sin.
- Frame devices: dreams, the narrator as traveller, the "twice-told" tale.
