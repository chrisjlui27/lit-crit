## Devices to expect in Bradstreet items

Heroic couplets, iambic tetrameter and pentameter, apostrophe, extended
metaphor and conceit, personification (Flesh and Spirit), allegory,
inversion for meter, elision, alliteration, biblical and classical
allusion, the modesty topos, carpe diem and vanitas themes, ubi sunt.
