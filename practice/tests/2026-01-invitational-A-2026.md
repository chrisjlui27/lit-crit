# Test log: UIL Literary Criticism Invitational A 2026

Source: `resources/UIL-LitCrit-Invitational-A-2026-sample-test.pdf` (test,
key, and essay judging notes). This was last season's Invitational A, so
Part 2 covers the 2025-26 list (*The Iceman Cometh*, *To Kill a
Mockingbird*, Shakespeare's sonnets) and is not useful for our students
except as a model of item style.

Use: Parts 1 and 3 as the baseline diagnostic (45 items, 60 points, give
45 minutes) and the essay prompt as the first essay assignment.
