# Most-tested Part 1 terms, 2009-2026

Generated from `terms/tested-terms-2009-2026.csv`, which is parsed from the UIL
master list of tested terms (`resources/UIL-LitCrit-master-list-of-terms-2026-07.pdf`).
Each row in the CSV is one Part 1 item: the meet, the correct answer, and the four
distractors. Boldface in the PDF marks answers to NOT items; that formatting is lost
in the CSV.

- Items indexed: 3646 across 18 seasons (five meets a season).
- Distinct correct answers: 890.
- Tested five or more times: 221 terms. Three or four times: 138. Twice: 164. Once: 367.
- The 221 terms tested five or more times account for 68% of all items; adding the three-and-four-time terms covers 81%.

## How to use this

- Tier 1 (5+ tests) is the first terms batch of the season. Every student should know
  every one of these cold, with an example, before Invitational A.
- Tier 2 (3-4 tests) is batches two through four.
- Tier 3 (tested twice) and the long tail are second-semester material, learned
  alongside the full glossary pass rather than instead of it.
- Distractors matter: an item's wrong answers are usually the same-family terms
  (kinds of rhyme, kinds of sonnet, literary periods). Drill the family, not the word.
