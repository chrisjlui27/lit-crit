# Tie-breaker essay rubric

Judges compare tied essays on three criteria in this order. Score each
1-4 for practice; a 12 is a model essay.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Response to the prompt | off-topic or summary only | addresses the prompt in part | answers the prompt directly with a clear claim | answers precisely, with a claim that governs the whole essay |
| Depth of literary analysis | names devices without effect | explains one effect | connects devices to meaning with evidence | sustained close reading; quotes and explains how form makes meaning |
| Quality of expression | hard to follow | clear but plain | controlled, varied, precise | fluent and economical; every sentence does work |

## Twenty-minute plan

1. Read the passage twice. Underline the three details you will use.
2. One-sentence claim that answers the prompt.
3. Three short paragraphs: one detail each, quote it, say what it does.
4. One-sentence close that returns to the claim.
5. Skip the introduction paragraph; start with the claim.

## Common failures

- Retelling the passage.
- Listing devices ("The author uses imagery, diction, and tone") without
  connecting any to the prompt.
- Running out of time before the ending.
