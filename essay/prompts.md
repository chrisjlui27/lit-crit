# Essay prompts

Add prompts as they are used. UIL prompts give a short passage (a poem or a
prose paragraph) and ask a focused question about how it works.

## Prompt patterns to practice

- How does the speaker's attitude shift across the poem, and what signals it?
- Discuss how the form (sonnet, ballad, blank verse) serves the meaning.
- How does the passage characterize X through diction and syntax?
- What is the effect of the final line, given what precedes it?
