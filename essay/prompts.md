# Essay prompts

Add prompts as they are used. UIL prompts print a short passage (a poem or
a prose paragraph), often with glosses, and ask one focused question.

## Prompt patterns to practice

- How does the speaker's attitude shift, and what in the closing lines
  signals it?
- How does the form (sonnet, couplets, blank verse) serve the meaning?
- How does the passage characterize X through diction and syntax?
- What is the effect of the final line, given what precedes it?
- How does a single controlling image organize the poem?

## Bradstreet prompts for this season

Public domain, so they can be printed in full.

- "The Author to Her Book": discuss how the controlling metaphor of the
  book as a child shapes the speaker's attitude toward her own work.
- "To My Dear and Loving Husband": discuss how the final couplet changes
  the poem's sense of time.
- "The Flesh and the Spirit": discuss how the two speakers are
  distinguished by diction.
- "Before the Birth of One of Her Children": discuss the speaker's
  attitude toward death as it develops across the poem.
