# Lit Crit

Coaching workspace for a high-school **UIL Literary Criticism** team,
2026-27 season. Everything here is planning material: the season plan,
study tracks, practice sets, essay work, and progress trackers.

Start with:

1. `season/season-plan.md` for the arc of the year.
2. `season/calendar.md` and fill in the dates from the official UIL calendar.
3. `reading-list/2026-2027.md` and fill in this year's three works and
   required editions.
4. `contest/format.md` to brief new students on the exam.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the layout and conventions, including the rule against
committing student data.
